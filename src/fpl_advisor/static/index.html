Where do I find these?

Team ID. Open the FPL website or app and go to the Points page. The link contains /entry/1234567/. That number is your team ID. You can paste the whole link.

League ID. Open your mini-league's standings. The link contains /leagues/76543/. Paste that link or the number.

In the app, use Share or copy the link from the browser version. The numbers above are examples.
